A frame is a bordered panel with two lime crop marks, top left and bottom right.

## Use

Add `sf-frame` to the element that holds the sealed thing. It draws the `border` edge, the `card` ground and the two marks. Sections inside it are separated by `border` hairlines.

## Rules

- The marks mean "this is the object being sealed": the hero seal, the product shot, the sandbox log, the scan field, the status card of a target.
- One or two frames per screen. A plain panel is a bordered `card` without marks.
- Do not add a shadow or a radius.
- The marks are `positive`, never another color.
